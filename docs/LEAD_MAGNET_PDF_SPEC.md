## §A. Alternatives evaluation

The principal asked: should the first lead magnet target Eastern Fort Lauderdale luxury buyers, expired listings, absentee luxury owners, seller valuation, or waterfront buyers? Below is my evaluation against 6 dimensions, with a recommendation.

### Recommended primary: **E. Eastern Fort Lauderdale Luxury WATERFRONT Buyer's Guide**

**Why E over the principal's stated A:**
- Same audience size (waterfront is the dominant Fort Lauderdale luxury segment, not a narrow slice)
- Higher conversion intent (waterfront buyers self-select on a specific decision criterion vs. a general "luxury buyer")
- Differentiated where A is saturated — every Fort Lauderdale realtor offers a generic luxury buyer's guide; a WATERFRONT-specific guide with lot profiles, dockage capacity, fixed-bridge access, seawall age, and insurance considerations is uncommonly rigorous
- Pairs naturally with Mia's existing `/insights/lighthouse-point-lot-profiles` essay (verifiable site asset) for cross-link authority
- Honors the principal's stated direction (Eastern Fort Lauderdale + Buyer's Guide) while sharpening the differentiator

**Why A over the niche alternatives B/C:**
- B (Expired Listings) and C (Absentee Owners) require Mia to commit to specific positioning her site doesn't yet present. Building those positions requires a new buyers/sellers sub-page + schema + topic-cluster work. Doable in a future cycle but not the FIRST lead magnet.
- D (Seller Valuation) is more naturally a CMA tool than a downloadable guide — fits better as a future enhancement of `/valuation/` rather than a PDF-and-gated-download flow.

**If the principal disagrees and wants A pure (no waterfront sharpener):** rename to `The Eastern Fort Lauderdale Luxury Buyer's Guide` and broaden Section 3 (waterfront-specific content) to cover all luxury-buyer concerns equally. Everything else in this spec carries over.

## §B. PRIMARY SPEC — Eastern Fort Lauderdale Luxury Waterfront Buyer's Guide

### Target audience

- **Primary demographic:** $1M+ household income, 35-65 yo, considering OR actively shopping a $1M-$8M waterfront residence in Eastern Fort Lauderdale (Las Olas Isles, Harbor Beach, Bay Colony, Rio Vista, Coral Ridge waterfront streets), Eastern Boca Raton (Royal Palm Yacht & Country Club, The Sanctuary, Boca Bay Colony), or Eastern Delray Beach (Seagate, Gulf Stream, Intracoastal corridors).
- **Secondary demographic:** out-of-state buyers exploring Florida residency (NY, NJ, CT, MA, IL, CA), often purchasing remotely or via short FL visits.
- **Tertiary demographic:** existing FL homeowners considering a luxury upgrade or move within Eastern SE Florida.
- **Pain points addressed:** lot profile literacy (canal vs. point vs. lakefront vs. ocean-access), dockage capacity vs. yacht size, fixed-bridge clearance, seawall age and FEMA flood-zone implications, insurance markets post-2024 reforms, dual-agency vs. exclusive-buyer-rep, off-market access, contract-to-close timeline expectations.

### Positioning

- **Tone:** editorial luxury, not transactional realtor-template. First-person Mia voice on key passages ("In my practice, I've found..."), third-person elsewhere.
- **Voice anchors:** echoes `MIA.voice.tagline` ("Fort Lauderdale REALTOR® | Waterfront, Luxury, and Family Homes Where Memories Are Made") + anchor line ("If I don't know the answer, I will find it.")
- **Positioning statement:** "A working primer for the buyer who wants to know the residence — and the water it sits on — before scheduling a showing."
- **Anti-positioning:** NOT a "10 tips for buying luxury real estate" listicle. NOT a SEO doorway pamphlet. NOT a generic FL real estate market summary. The guide is rigorous, neighborhood-anchored, and assumes a sophisticated reader.

### Table of contents (8 sections, ~16-24 pages PDF)

1. **The Eastern SE Florida Luxury Waterfront Map** — orientation: Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, plus the canal/finger-isle systems that define luxury inventory.
2. **Lot-Profile Literacy** — point lots vs. fingers vs. canals vs. lakes vs. ocean-access; what each means for yacht access, view, lot value, resale dynamics. (Pulls from `/insights/lighthouse-point-lot-profiles` essay; expands.)
3. **Dockage, Bridges, and the Hillsboro Inlet** — fixed-bridge clearance constraints; deep-water no-fixed-bridge dockage premium; Hillsboro vs. Port Everglades vs. Boca Inlet ocean access; how to read a listing's "dockage" claim.
4. **Seawall, Elevation, and FEMA Flood Zones** — what age the seawall and what condition; FEMA Zone AE/X/VE implications for insurance + financing; post-2024 Florida insurance market realities.
5. **The 5-Step Eastern Fort Lauderdale Waterfront Search** — Mia's actual workflow: brief, lot-criteria filter, off-market access, showing strategy, due diligence, contract-to-close.
6. **Five Eastern Markets, Side-by-Side** — comparison table: Fort Lauderdale (Las Olas Isles + Harbor Beach + Bay Colony + Rio Vista), Coral Ridge waterfront, Victoria Park (in-town vs. waterfront), Boca Raton (Royal Palm + The Sanctuary + St. Andrews), Delray Beach (Seagate + Gulf Stream + Intracoastal). One paragraph + lifestyle pillars + price character per market. (Pulls from `markets.ts` + `/insights/` essays.)
7. **The Off-Market Question** — when off-market matters, why exclusive buyer rep is the access vector, how Mia's deliberately-small-client-list approach makes off-market introductions possible.
8. **Working with Mia: What to Expect** — practice structure, communication cadence, fee approach (no specifics — "discussed in private consultation"), the consultation process. CTA: "Schedule a private consultation."

**Page-count target:** 16-24 PDF pages at 8.5"×11" or A4, depending on imagery + spacing density. Editorial-luxury feel — not crammed.

### Section-by-section copy outline

#### Section 1 — Map (2-3 pages)

- Opening: "Eastern Southeast Florida's luxury waterfront sits on a small set of canal and finger-isle systems. The geography is the inventory."
- Map graphic: Eastern Fort Lauderdale + Eastern Boca + Eastern Delray with canal systems annotated.
- 3-paragraph orientation: water access matters more than square footage; the canal you sit on shapes resale dynamics; understanding the geography is the first prerequisite.
- Cross-link references at footer: "See Section 6 for a market-by-market comparison."

#### Section 2 — Lot-Profile Literacy (3-4 pages)

- Define 6 lot types: point, finger isle, canal-wide, lake/lagoon, ocean-access, and inland.
- One paragraph + 1 representative graphic per type.
- "How to read a listing's lot description" subsection — terms vendors use vs. what they actually mean.
- Pulls language from the existing `/insights/lighthouse-point-lot-profiles` essay (cross-link: "For Lighthouse Point's specific lot dynamics, see [insights essay link]").

#### Section 3 — Dockage, Bridges, Inlets (3 pages)

- Define fixed-bridge constraint and how it caps yacht dimensions
- Hillsboro Inlet (Lighthouse Point ocean access — no fixed bridges) vs. Port Everglades (Fort Lauderdale main inlet) vs. Boca Inlet
- "Reading a dockage claim" — capacity vs. depth vs. clearance vs. routing time
- Pull-quote from `/insights/lighthouse-point-lot-profiles` if § 1-verifiable.

#### Section 4 — Seawall, Elevation, FEMA (2-3 pages)

- Seawall age + condition + replacement cost
- FEMA zones AE/X/VE — what each means for insurance + financing
- Post-2024 FL insurance market realities — Citizens Property Insurance, Florida-domiciled carrier exits, surplus-lines markets
- Diligence checklist: "Questions to ask before contract."

#### Section 5 — The 5-Step Search Workflow (2 pages)

- Step 1: Brief (private consultation; lifestyle, timeline, lot criteria)
- Step 2: Filter + off-market introduction (Mia's network access)
- Step 3: Showing strategy (in-person + remote-buyer accommodation)
- Step 4: Due diligence (title, structural, environmental, dockage)
- Step 5: Contract-to-close (timeline expectations, parallel coordination of inspections / insurance / financing / title)

#### Section 6 — Five Eastern Markets, Side-by-Side (3-4 pages)

- Table: Market | Lifestyle pillars | Price character | Lot inventory | Notes
- One paragraph per market echoing `markets.ts` `localContext` field
- §1-verified `miaQuote` from each market where present (Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Palm Beach, Delray Beach, Lighthouse Point per `markets.ts`; Coral Ridge + Victoria Park no quote available — surface as "Mia speaks to this market in private consultation.")

#### Section 7 — The Off-Market Question (1-2 pages)

- Why off-market matters in luxury (privacy, speed, supply-side pressure)
- How exclusive buyer rep enables it (network access, brokerage reciprocity, agent-to-agent introductions)
- Mia's deliberately-small-client-list approach (echoes about-page "Personal by design, not by claim.")

#### Section 8 — Working with Mia (1-2 pages)

- Practice structure (small client list; full presence)
- Communication cadence (response window, weekly cadence during active search, post-close relationship)
- Fee approach (kept abstract: "discussed in private consultation"; no specific commission claims per FREC § 61J2-10.025)
