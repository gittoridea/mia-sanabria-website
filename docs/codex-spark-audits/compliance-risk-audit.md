## Florida-specific compliance findings
- FREC 61J2-10.025 requires the brokerage firm name on all advertising and, on websites, adjacent to or immediately above/below point-of-contact information. The repo satisfies this in some identity/footer contexts but not consistently at direct contact/form modules.
- Florida does not require a license number in the ad rule itself; displaying an unverified one creates risk without adding required compliance value.
- The footer’s `BROKERAGE` address block currently reads like a location disclosure even though `displayOffice` is null and the shown city/ZIP comes from service-core data, not a confirmed office.
- Fla. Stat. § 501.171 breach-notice coverage is present in the privacy policy; Fla. Stat. § 501.059 submission-point consent is not.

## Federal-specific compliance findings (TCPA / Fair Housing / DMCA)
- TCPA/lead-gen consent is not adequate at the form itself; terms-page language is not a substitute for clear, conspicuous, point-of-submission consent if phone/SMS/autodialed follow-up is contemplated.
- Fair Housing coverage is strong: footer EHO display and terms-page non-discrimination language are both present and should stay.
- The DMCA page is procedurally incomplete because it still says registration is in progress and omits final designated-agent publication details.
