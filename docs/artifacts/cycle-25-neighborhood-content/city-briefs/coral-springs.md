## Page angle

Northwestern Broward planned residential city, originally master-planned by Coral Ridge Properties in the early 1960s. Established single-family residential character on a deliberate road grid with a wide civic-and-parks footprint.

## Target visitor

- Northwestern-Broward buyer comparing Coral Springs to Parkland, Sunrise, Plantation, or Weston
- Seller of an established single-family residence in one of the original Coral Springs subdivisions
- Relocating buyer who wants a planned-community character over a beach-corridor lifestyle
- Absentee owner with a Coral Springs property in their portfolio

## Local positioning

- Northwestern Broward municipality, bordered by Parkland to the north, Tamarac and Margate to the south/east, and Coconut Creek to the east
- Named for the original developer Coral Ridge Properties' "Coral Springs" master plan filed in 1963; incorporated 1963
- Sample Road / University Drive / Atlantic Boulevard axis as the major civic and commercial spine
- Established city parks system; Sportsplex and city tennis center are publicly named municipal facilities
- Predominantly inland single-family residential character; no Intracoastal or Atlantic frontage
