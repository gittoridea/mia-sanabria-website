## Page angle

South Broward coastal city, framed by Hallandale Beach to the south, Dania Beach to the north, and the Atlantic on the east. Distinct from Los Angeles' Hollywood; geographic anchor in the opening sentence is mandatory. Hollywood Broadwalk, Young Circle / ArtsPark, and the Intracoastal Waterway form the three identity anchors.

## Target visitor

- South-Broward beachfront or beach-corridor buyer comparing Hollywood to Pompano Beach, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea, Lauderdale Lakes
- Seller of an Intracoastal-side or beach-corridor residence in central or eastern Hollywood
- Relocating buyer prioritizing walkable-downtown + beach access
- Buyer seeking lower entry into a coastal Broward city than the Eastern Fort Lauderdale cohort offers

## Local positioning

- South Broward coastal municipality, incorporated 1925
- Founded by Joseph W. Young; developed as a Florida-coast city in the 1920s land-boom era
- Hollywood Broadwalk — the public oceanfront pedestrian promenade running south from the Hollywood Beach pier area, named landmark
- Young Circle (downtown) and ArtsPark at Young Circle — named civic landmarks
- Intracoastal Waterway runs through the city; the residential mix includes single-family on canal-routed lots and condominium on the A1A corridor
- Bordered by Hallandale Beach (south), Dania Beach (north), West Park (west)
