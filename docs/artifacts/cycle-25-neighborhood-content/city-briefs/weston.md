## Page angle

Western Broward master-planned community, developed by Arvida beginning in the 1980s on land at the western edge of Broward against the Everglades Conservation Area. Gated-community character, low-density residential, parkway-style road grid.

## Target visitor

- Western-Broward buyer comparing Weston to Davie, Sunrise, Parkland
- Seller of an established single-family residence in one of the named Weston communities (Weston Hills, the Falls, Country Isles, Bonaventure, etc.)
- Gated-community seeker prioritizing master-planned character
- Relocating buyer wanting western-Broward residential density at the edge of the metropolitan area

## Local positioning

- Western Broward municipality, incorporated 1996
- Developed beginning in the 1980s by Arvida (originally the Arvida/JMB Partners "Weston" master plan); incorporated as the city of Weston in 1996
- Located at the western edge of urbanized Broward, bounded on the west by the Everglades Water Conservation Area
- Master-planned road grid centered on Royal Palm Boulevard / Indian Trace / Bonaventure Boulevard / Saddle Club Road
- Named Weston communities (Weston Hills, the Falls, Country Isles, Bonaventure, Savanna, Windmill Ranch Estates) — each is its own community within the city
