Notes from the Southeast Florida luxury and waterfront market.

Twelve editorial briefs — buyer guides, market comparisons, neighborhood reads, and pre-listing positioning. Read in any order; each is written for a specific decision.

The Library

A twelve-part editorial library.

These briefs are written for the buyers and sellers Mia works with directly — waterfront and luxury across Eastern Fort Lauderdale and the adjacent Boca Raton and Delray Beach corridors. Each brief is built to make the first private conversation more productive. It frames what to verify on a specific address, how to separate similar markets, and where the leverage points sit before any introduction.

The library is a guide series, not a chronological dispatch. The topic-month labels organize the twelve briefs as a working market cycle; you can read in any order, and each brief stands alone.
