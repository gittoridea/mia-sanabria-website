## City identity

Hollywood is a south Broward coastal city (distinct from Los Angeles), formally incorporated November 28, 1925. Founded by Joseph W. Young — the Joseph W. Young House is on the National Register of Historic Places. Bordered by Hallandale Beach (south), Dania Beach (north), West Park (west). Named civic anchors: Hollywood Broadwalk (public oceanfront pedestrian promenade) ; Young Circle / ArtsPark at Young Circle.
