## City identity

Deerfield Beach is a northeastern Broward coastal city bordered by Boca Raton (Palm Beach County) to the north and Pompano Beach + Lighthouse Point to the south. Approximately 17 sq mi. The market pairs a continuous public oceanfront and the Deerfield Beach Pier with the Hillsboro Inlet at its southern edge, the Cove residential pocket along the Intracoastal in the northeast, and the 430-acre Quiet Waters Park (Broward County Parks).

- Hillsboro Inlet — navigable inlet at the southern edge of Deerfield Beach (route to Atlantic for canal-routed homes).
- Major road corridors: Hillsboro Boulevard / Federal Highway / A1A.
