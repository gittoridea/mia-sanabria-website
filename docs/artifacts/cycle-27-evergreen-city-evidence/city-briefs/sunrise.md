## City identity

Sunrise is a western Broward city incorporated 1961, originally as Sunrise Golf Village (founded by Norman Johnson per the city's official history). Named civic anchors: Sawgrass Mills retail district (opened 1990 ; one of the largest retail destinations nationally) and the BB&T Center / Amerant Bank Arena (home of the NHL Florida Panthers ; renamed Amerant Bank Arena September 19, 2023). Expressway access: Sawgrass Expressway, Florida Turnpike, I-595.
