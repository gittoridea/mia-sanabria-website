## City identity

Plantation is a central Broward city incorporated April 23, 1953. Bordered by Sunrise to the north, Davie to the south, and Fort Lauderdale to the east. Major road corridors: Broward Boulevard, University Drive, Sunrise Boulevard, Peters Road. Named civic anchors include Plantation Heritage Park (Broward Parks system) and Volunteer Park (city facility).
