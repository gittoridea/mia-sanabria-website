## City identity

Weston is a western Broward city incorporated September 3, 1996. Developed beginning in the 1980s by Arvida (formally Arvida/JMB Realty) under the Weston master plan, with the first homes completed in 1984 in Windmill Ranch and Country Isles. Bounded on the west by the Everglades Water Conservation Area. Named Weston communities include Weston Hills, the Falls, Country Isles, Bonaventure, Savanna, and Windmill Ranch Estates. Parkway-style road grid: Royal Palm Boulevard, Indian Trace, Bonaventure Boulevard, Saddle Club Road.
