## City identity

Davie is a central Broward town first incorporated 1925, re-incorporated 1961 — the prior incarnation having been dissolved during the Great Depression with the 1961 reincorporation establishing the modern town government. Davie is recognized as a Tree City USA (continuously since 1991 per the Arbor Day Foundation; 2017 Tree City USA recipient per the town's own news). The Equestrian Trail network is maintained by the Town. College corridor includes Nova Southeastern University (NSU), Broward College Central Campus, and FAU Davie. Bordered by Plantation (north) and Pembroke Pines (south).
