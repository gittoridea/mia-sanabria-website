## Geography

Coastal city on the Atlantic Ocean in northern Broward County, roughly 40 miles north of Miami in the Miami metropolitan area. The mainland is separated from its Atlantic barrier island (Deerfield Beach Island) by the Intracoastal Waterway. Bordered by the Hillsboro River/Canal and Boca Raton (Palm Beach County) to the north, Pompano Beach to the south, and Coconut Creek inland to the west. Ocean access for boaters is via the Hillsboro Inlet (south) and the Boca Raton Inlet (north). **[verified]** — Wikipedia; Britannica ("city in Broward County, southeastern Florida, on the Atlantic Ocean, just south of Boca Raton"); Greater Fort Lauderdale Chamber.

> **County / coastal sanity check (explicit):** Deerfield Beach is correctly placed in **Broward County** (NOT Palm Beach, even though Boca Raton/Palm Beach County borders it to the north, and NOT Miami-Dade) and is correctly described as **coastal** — direct Atlantic Ocean frontage on the barrier island plus Intracoastal Waterway frontage on the mainland. Both pass. **[verified]** — Wikipedia, Britannica.

## Confirmed Durable Facts

1. Deerfield Beach is a coastal city in **Broward County, Florida**, in the Miami metropolitan area, roughly **40 miles north of Miami**, just south of Boca Raton. **[verified]** — Britannica; Wikipedia.
2. It is bordered by the **Atlantic Ocean to the east, the Hillsboro River/Canal and Boca Raton (Palm Beach County) to the north, Pompano Beach to the south, and Coconut Creek to the west**. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; Britannica (just south of Boca Raton).
3. The **Intracoastal Waterway separates the mainland from the Atlantic barrier island, Deerfield Beach Island**. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; Britannica ("boardwalk along Ocean Way and the Deerfield Beach International Fishing Pier on Deerfield Beach Island").
4. Ocean access for boaters is via the **Hillsboro Inlet to the south and the Boca Raton Inlet to the north**. **[verified]** — bythesearealty (The Cove "located between the Boca Raton Inlet and the Hillsboro Inlet"); marinaone.com; thecovedeerfield.com.
5. Major corridors include **I-95, US-1/Federal Highway, Hillsboro Boulevard (main east-west route), Dixie Highway, Powerline Road, and A1A on the barrier island**. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; multiple sources (Century Village main gate on Hillsboro Blvd; Marina One at 580 N Federal Hwy; Cove between US-1 and the Intracoastal). **[evergreen-general]** for the regional arterial framing.
6. **The Cove** is the signature waterfront/boating neighborhood — 1,000+ single-family homes on protected canals (20 finger canals) and the Intracoastal, between US-1 and the waterway from Hillsboro Blvd south toward Lighthouse Point, with no-fixed-bridge ocean access. **[verified]** — bythesearealty; thecoverealestate.net; thecovedeerfield.com.
7. **Deerfield Beach Island** holds the public beach, boardwalk, the **~976-foot International Fishing Pier** (the longest pier in Broward County, at 200 NE 21st Ave), and oceanfront condominium towers. **[verified]** — deerfield-beach.com (official); visitlauderdale; reddotpier.
8. The city's public beach has earned **Blue Wave beach certification** from the Clean Beaches Coalition (Blue Wave certified since 1999). **[verified]** — Clean Beaches Coalition; movoto; deerfield-beach.com.
9. **Deerfield Island Park** is a **~53-acre county nature preserve accessible only by boat** via a **free shuttle from Sullivan Park Marina** (Fri-Sun, 10am-3pm). **[verified]** — Broward County Parks (broward.org); TripAdvisor; wildsouthflorida; miamionthecheap (free shuttle from Sullivan Park, 1701 Riverview Road).
10. **Quiet Waters Park** is a large (~430-acre) Broward County park known for the **Ski Rixen cable water-ski** system; **Constitution Park** houses the **Deerfield Beach Arboretum ("Tree Zoo")**, ~9 acres, with **200+ exotic tree species** (founded 1995, since grown to 300+ non-native species). **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Quiet Waters Park, Deerfield Beach Arboretum); deerfield-beach.com; thetreezoo.com. *(Dossier's "200+" is conservative — current source figure is ~325 species; "200+" remains accurate.)*
11. **Deer Creek** is an inland golf community built around the **Deer Creek Golf Club** (18-hole Arthur Hills course, est. 1971), about a 10-minute drive from the beach. **[verified]** — deercreekflorida.com; PGA.com; GolfPass.
12. **Century Village** (Century Village East) is a large gated **55+ active-adult condominium community** (8,508 units on ~750 acres, main gate on Hillsboro Blvd, 145,000 sq ft clubhouse); **Crystal Lake** includes **55+ villa/condo product** (The Meadows of Crystal Lake, Crystal Lake 4100, Veleiros at Crystal Lake). **[verified]** — centuryvillage.com; 55places.com; activeadultliving.com. *(See housing-stock nuance below: Crystal Lake as a whole is mixed-age, not exclusively 55+.)*
13. **The Cove Marina** (full-service, expanded to **~400 ft**, on the Intracoastal at 1756 SE 3rd Court, connected to The Cove Waterfront Restaurant) and **Marina One** (on the Hillsboro Canal between Boca Raton and Deerfield Beach, 580 N Federal Hwy, dry-rack + in-water storage, ~15 min by boat to the Boca Raton Inlet) serve area boaters. **[verified]** — thecovedeerfield.com; marinaone.com.
14. **JB's On The Beach** (oceanfront seafood, 300 NE 21st Ave, opened October 2002) and **The Cove Waterfront Restaurant and Tiki Bar** (at The Cove Marina) are established dining landmarks. **[verified]** — jbsonthebeach.com; Yelp (300 NE 21st Ave); thecovedeerfield.com.
15. Many of the city's oldest structures, built in the **1920s, are in a Spanish Mediterranean Revival style**; the **1926 railway station survives** (built by the Seaboard Air Line Railway, Mediterranean Revival, on the National Register of Historic Places). **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Deerfield Beach station); Great American Stations. *(Note: the surviving 1926 station was built by the Seaboard Air Line Railway, not the FEC — see fact 16; the dossier does not conflate them.)*
16. The **Florida East Coast Railway reached the area in 1896**; the **Town of Deerfield was incorporated June 11, 1925** (renamed Deerfield Beach in 1939). **[verified]** — Britannica (FEC 1896; incorporated as Deerfield 1925); deerfield-beach.com History; Wikipedia ("Town of Deerfield incorporated on June 11, 1925").
