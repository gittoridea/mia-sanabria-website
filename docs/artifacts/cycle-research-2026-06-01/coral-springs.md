## Confirmed Durable Facts

1. Coral Springs is in Broward County, FL, roughly 20 miles northwest of Fort Lauderdale, within the Miami metropolitan area. [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Springs,_Florida]
2. Inland, landlocked master-planned city of ~24 square miles in the northwest corner of Broward County, bordering the Everglades to the west. [verified — en.wikipedia.org; U.S. Census QuickFacts]
3. Borders Parkland (north), Coconut Creek (east), Margate and North Lauderdale (southeast), Tamarac (south), and the Everglades (west). [verified — en.wikipedia.org; coralspringsfl.com/about-coral-springs/where-is-coral-springs]
4. Master-planned and developed by Coral Ridge Properties, Inc. (acquired by Westinghouse in 1966) and officially chartered on July 10, 1963. [verified — en.wikipedia.org; coralsprings.gov history]
5. Despite its name there are no natural springs in the city; the land was drained marsh shaped by canals and levees. [verified — en.wikipedia.org]
6. The Sawgrass Expressway (SR 869) borders the city's northern and western edges; principal corridors include University Drive, Atlantic Boulevard, Sample Road, and Wiles Road. [verified — en.wikipedia.org; corridor names confirmed across city/visitor sources]
7. No Intracoastal or ocean access; inland boating/fishing is via the Cypress Creek Canal (C-14) system and man-made community lakes, with public boat ramps at University Drive and Coral Ridge Drive (plus a Rock Island Road ramp downstream in the regional system). [verified — boatrampguide.com (Cypress Creek Canal C-14); myfwc.com C-14 angler guide]
8. The city maintains over 50 parks totaling 675+ acres, including Mullins Park (~70 acres, the largest park) and the Sandy Ridge Sanctuary conservation area (~38 acres, largest natural conservation area). [verified — coralsprings.gov Parks-Directory (Mullins Park, Sandy Ridge); city Parks & Rec dept page]
9. Golf venues include the Country Club of Coral Springs (public/semi-private, opened 1969, on West Sample Road) and TPC at Eagle Trace (opened 1983), which hosted the PGA Tour's Honda Classic (1984–1991 and 1996). [verified — ccofcs.com; golfpass.com; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle_Trace_Golf_Club; pga.com]
10. The former Heron Bay golf course (formerly TPC at Heron Bay) closed in 2019; the ~223-acre site was sold to the North Springs Improvement District (regional water utility) and is largely being converted to a stormwater-management area with walking trails — no longer an active golf course. [verified — globalgolfpost.com "The disappearance of Heron Bay"; coralspringstalk.com]
11. Named residential communities include Heron Bay, Eagle Trace, Maplewood Isle, The Isles (at Lake Coral Springs), Whispering Woods, Ramblewood, Wyndham Lakes, Kensington, Coral Springs Lakes, and the central Coral Springs City Center. [verified — floridahomefinder.com neighborhood guides; premierlistings.com (The Isles at Lake Coral Springs); Redfin neighborhood pages for City Center and Coral Springs Lakes]
12. Cultural/civic landmarks include the Coral Springs Center for the Arts (1,471-seat theatre), the Coral Springs Museum of Art, The Walk of Coral Springs (open-air retail/dining on University Drive), Coral Square mall (regional enclosed mall, opened 1984, Atlantic Blvd & University Drive), and the 1964 Coral Springs Covered Bridge. [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Springs_Center_for_the_Arts; thewalkofcoralsprings.com; simon.com/mall/coral-square; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Springs_Covered_Bridge]
13. The Coral Springs Covered Bridge (1964) is the only covered bridge in Florida in a public right-of-way (and a designated Florida Heritage Site). [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Springs_Covered_Bridge; coralsprings.gov/Residents/Our-Community/Coral-Springs-History/Covered-Bridge]
14. 2020 census population: 134,394. [verified — en.wikipedia.org; U.S. Census QuickFacts] [evergreen-general — cite as 2020 census, not "current"]

- **TPC at Eagle Trace naming:** The course is now branded "Eagle Trace Golf Club" (formerly TPC at Eagle Trace). The dossier's "former PGA Tour Honda Classic venue" is accurate — it hosted the Honda Classic 1984–1991 and again in 1996. The "TPC at Eagle Trace" label is acceptable as a historical/known name; if publishing current copy, "Eagle Trace Golf Club (former TPC at Eagle Trace)" is the most precise.
- **Heron Bay golf:** The dossier's sub-area note says "its former TPC golf course closed in 2019." Confirmed; the 2021 reference in `durableFacts` ("officially announced 2021") reflects the formal wind-down — both consistent. Safe to publish as "former golf course (closed 2019), now a stormwater-management area with trails."
- **Sandy Ridge Sanctuary acreage:** Confirmed ~38 acres (one query auto-suggested "not 50" against a strawman; the dossier's 38 figure is correct).

## Confirmed Sub-Areas (character lines — strictly physical/amenity, Fair-Housing-clean)

- **Heron Bay** — Guard-gated master-planned community in the northwest corner bordering Parkland, with 30+ sub-neighborhoods, resort-style clubhouses, pools, fitness centers, tennis, and walking paths (its former golf course closed in 2019). [verified]
- **Eagle Trace** — Gated golf community built around the Eagle Trace / former TPC at Eagle Trace course (a former Honda Classic venue), with single-family homes, townhomes, and villas set along fairways and lakes. [verified]
- **Maplewood Isle** — Gated single-family neighborhood with lakefront lots, mature landscaping, and water views. [verified]
- **The Isles** — Lakefront community with many homes on the water's edge overlooking Lake Coral Springs. [verified — The Isles at Lake Coral Springs, gated lake community]
- **Whispering Woods** — Community set on a private lake with a clubhouse, tennis courts, swimming pool, and spa. [verified — echofineproperties.com; floridahomefinder.com]
- **Ramblewood** — Established single-family neighborhood with tree-lined streets and well-maintained homes. [verified]
- **Wyndham Lakes** — Lake-oriented residential community in Coral Springs. [verified — represented in Coral Springs community listings]
- **Kensington** — Established single-family residential neighborhood in Coral Springs. [verified — represented in Coral Springs community listings]
- **Coral Springs City Center** — Central, more urbanized district near the civic/cultural core, with a mix of attached housing and proximity to the arts center and downtown amenities. [verified — Redfin neighborhood page: Coral-Springs-City-Center]
- **Coral Springs Lakes** — Residential community organized around man-made lakes with water-view lots. [verified — Redfin neighborhood page: Coral-Springs-Lakes]
