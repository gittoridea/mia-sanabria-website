## Geography

Coastal city in northeastern Broward County on the Atlantic Ocean, just north of Fort Lauderdale and about 36 miles north of Miami (principal city of the Miami metropolitan area). It has roughly 3 miles of public Atlantic beachfront extending from the intersection of State Road A1A and Terra Mar Drive to the Hillsboro Inlet. The Intracoastal Waterway runs through the city between the mainland and the barrier beach; the Hillsboro Inlet (part of the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway) provides direct ocean access. The city spans both barrier/beachside areas and a larger inland mainland with canal-laced waterfront pockets. **[verified]** — Wikipedia, Britannica, City of Pompano Beach.

## Confirmed Durable Facts

1. Pompano Beach is in Broward County, Florida, on the Atlantic Ocean in the northeastern part of the county. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; Britannica.
2. It sits just north of Fort Lauderdale and roughly 36 miles north of Miami within the Miami metropolitan area. **[verified]** — Wikipedia.
3. The city has about 3 miles of public Atlantic beachfront, running from State Road A1A and Terra Mar Drive to the Hillsboro Inlet. **[verified]** — Wikipedia.
4. The Intracoastal Waterway runs through the city between the mainland and the barrier beach. **[verified]** — Wikipedia.
5. The Hillsboro Inlet provides direct Intracoastal-to-Atlantic ocean access and is a noted sport-fishing gateway; the inlet is shared with neighboring Hillsboro Beach and Lighthouse Point. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; florida-backroads-travel.
6. The Hillsboro Inlet Lighthouse (first lit March 1907) stands on the north side of the inlet in adjacent Hillsboro Beach. **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Hillsboro Inlet Light); Atlas Obscura. **CORRECTION:** do NOT publish "~136 ft tall" as tower height — 136 ft is the *focal height* of the light; Wikipedia lists the *tower* height as ~132 ft and other sources cite 142/147 ft. Publish "first lit 1907" only; omit a specific height figure.
7. Major corridors include State Road A1A, U.S. 1/Federal Highway, Atlantic Boulevard (SR 814), Sample Road, Dixie Highway, Interstate 95, and Florida's Turnpike (plus Copans Road and McNab Road). **[verified]** — Wikipedia; corridor names cross-checked against neighborhood/city sources.
8. The city recognizes many named neighborhoods (commonly cited as 30-plus / "42 fully or partially within"), including Old Pompano, Downtown Pompano Beach, Harbor Village, Hillsboro Shores, Terra Mar, Palm Aire, Cypress Bend, Cypress Cove, Snug Harbor, Pompano Beach Highlands, Cresthaven, Leisureville, Kendall Green, and Loch Lomond. **[verified]** — Homes.com (20+ listed), Trulia, ZeroDown, pompanobeachrealty.
9. Palm Aire is a large inland country-club community with four golf courses and multiple sub-communities (e.g., Vizcaya, Misty Oaks, Cypress Reach). **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Palm Aire); gngcd.
10. Housing stock ranges from Intracoastal and canal-front single-family homes with docks to oceanfront condo towers, mid-century homes, and country-club villas/estates. **[verified]** — neighborhood sources (Homes.com, pompanobeachrealty, Nextdoor).
11. The Fisher Family Pier (Pompano Beach Pier), originally built in 1963, was rebuilt/improved over 2019-2022 to over 900 feet with an elevated, widened deck (reopened 2020 after an ~$11.5M reconstruction). **[verified]** — City of Pompano Beach CRA; visitpompanobeach; parks.pompanobeachfl.gov.
12. The Pompano Beach Fishing Village is a six-acre redeveloped beachfront destination with dining, shops, a tiki bar, a dual-branded Hilton (Tru/Home2 Suites) hotel, and a parking garage. **[verified]** — City of Pompano Beach CRA; visitpompanobeach.
13. Sands Harbor Resort & Marina is a full-service marina near the Hillsboro Inlet offering dockage for vessels up to about 120-125 feet, with a fuel dock. **[verified]** — sandsharbor.com; marinas.com; Dockwa.
14. The Pompano Beach Golf Club includes the Greg Norman Signature Pines Course (reopened January 1, 2013) and the Palms Course, with an adjacent aquatic center. **[verified]** — gngcd; golfproperty; parks.pompanobeachfl.gov.
15. Harrah's Pompano Beach (formerly Isle Casino Racing Pompano Park) operates as a casino; the historic harness-racing track held its final race in April 2022 and the grandstand was subsequently demolished. **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Harrah's Pompano Beach); Local10 (demolition drone footage May 2023). **NOTE:** "demolished in 2022" is approximate — last race April 2022; physical demolition footage dated May 2023. Safe to say "closed in 2022 and demolished."
16. Boating is a defining feature, with canal-laced waterfront neighborhoods, private docks, and a public boat ramp at 14th Street. **[verified]** — marina/boating sources; consistent with city geography. **[evergreen-general]** for the canal/dock characterization.
17. The Old Pompano / Old Town arts-and-culture district (galleries, murals, Bailey Contemporary Arts / BaCA, near Dixie Highway & Atlantic Blvd) and the Civic Commons (Cultural Center, Library) anchor downtown CRA redevelopment. **[verified]** — pompanobeacharts.org; City of Pompano Beach.
