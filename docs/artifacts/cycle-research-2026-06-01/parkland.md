## Confirmed Durable Facts

1. Parkland is a city in **northern Broward County, Florida**, incorporated in 1963 (charter signed July 10, 1963), founded by farmer/rancher **Bruce Blount**, whose original parcels were marketed as "BBB Ranches." [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkland,_Florida; cityofparkland.org/1726/A-Brief-History-of-Parkland]
2. It is an **inland, fully landlocked** city — no ocean, inlet, beach, or Intracoastal Waterway frontage; the western edge abuts the Everglades / water conservation areas. [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkland,_Florida]
3. It is a **low-density, master-planned** community whose zoning protects a "park-like" character; the city brand/motto is **"Environmentally Proud"** (and it is a designated Tree City USA). [verified — cityofparkland.org/1572/The-City-of-Parkland-Brand; en.wikipedia.org]
4. **Equestrian heritage is central:** over the city's first decade, horses outnumbered the town's population; equestrian use continues at the **Equestrian Center at Temple Park** and on large-lot acreage in **Pine Tree Estates**. [verified — en.wikipedia.org (horses outnumbered population); cityofparkland.org Equestrian Center at Temple Park; parkland-florida-realty / homesofparkland (Pine Tree Estates horse-permitted deeds)]
5. Total area is about **12.8 square miles**, of which roughly **12.3 sq mi is land** (and ~0.50 sq mi water). [verified — census.gov QuickFacts (Parkland city, FL); consistent with the dossier]
6. The **Sawgrass Expressway (State Road 869)** runs along the city's southern boundary; **University Drive** (continuing unsigned through Parkland as a county road north of the Sawgrass) is the principal north-south corridor; **State Road 7 / US-441** lies to the east. [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_Road_869; floridasturnpike.com SR 869 project page; Grokipedia/AARoads SR 817 University Drive routing]
7. Major gated master-planned communities include **Heron Bay, Parkland Golf & Country Club, MiraLago, Cascata at MiraLago, Parkland Isles, Watercrest, and Parkland Bay.** [verified — parkland-florida-realty.com subdivisions; floridahomefinder.com gated-communities guide; Lennar resource center; community brokerage pages]
8. **Parkland Golf & Country Club** is built around a **Greg Norman-designed** 18-hole championship golf course (≈790-acre community, ~800 homes, with a sports/clubhouse complex). [verified — comeliveinflorida.com; parklandpowerteam.com; billsohlsellsparkland.com]
9. Housing "water" is **freshwater lakes and canals** within communities — lakefront / water-view lots, **not navigable saltwater**. [verified — en.wikipedia.org; community pages (Parkland Bay ~178-acre lake; Watercrest ~150 acres of lakes; MiraLago lake-oriented)]
10. **Five named retail/business centers** serve the city per the city's own list: **Waterways Shoppes, Parkland Commons, Parkland Town Center, Riverstone Shoppes, and Shoppes of Parkland.** [verified — cityofparkland.org/248/Business-Centers-in-Parkland; Yelp/commercial listings for Waterways Shoppes and Shoppes of Parkland (BJ's-anchored at SR 7 / Hillsboro Blvd)]
11. **Pine Trails Park** is the city's largest park (~77 acres) and home to the **Parkland Amphitheater**; **Terramar Park** features wetland boardwalks (and the **Doris Davis Forman Wilderness/Wildlife Preserve** has a ~900' pine-woods trail plus a ~1,550' cypress-wetland boardwalk). [verified — cityofparkland.org Pine Trails Park; cityofparkland.org Doris Davis Forman Wildlife Preserve; bestinparkland.com largest parks]
12. Parkland borders **Coconut Creek (east), Coral Springs (south),** and the **West Boca Raton area / Palm Beach County line to the north** (Everglades / water conservation areas to the west). [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkland,_Florida]
13. **2020 census population ≈ 34,670.** [verified — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkland,_Florida (2020 decennial)] [evergreen-general — cite as "2020 census," never as "current"]

## Confirmed Sub-Areas (character lines — strictly physical / architectural / amenity, Fair-Housing-clean)

- **Heron Bay** — Large gated master-planned community (1,000+ homes) with multiple sub-villages, two clubhouses, resort-style amenities, pools, tennis, an amenity island, and a mix of single-family homes, estates, and townhomes. [verified]
- **Parkland Golf & Country Club** — Gated golf community built around a Greg Norman-designed 18-hole championship course, with custom-built estate homes, a sports complex, tennis, and clubhouse dining. [verified]
- **MiraLago (Mira Lago)** — Gated community of Mediterranean-style single-family homes and townhomes, connected to sister community Cascata via an underground walkway beneath Nob Hill Road. [verified — Lennar resource center; comeliveinflorida; Homes.com guide]
- **Cascata at MiraLago** — Upscale gated enclave with contemporary/modern-style single-family homes and resort-style amenities (Club Cascata), sharing amenities with MiraLago via the underground walkway. [verified]
- **Parkland Isles** — Established gated community of single-family homes set among lakes and canals. [verified — en.wikipedia.org names it a large neighborhood development; brokerage subdivision pages]
- **Pine Tree Estates** — Rural-character community of custom homes on roughly one-to-three-acre lots with deeds permitting horses; barns, guest houses, workshops, and equestrian facilities are common; no formal HOA. [verified — homesofparkland.com; parklandparrot.com; dupreeteam.com]
- **Watercrest** — Luxury gated community of just under 600 (≈589) single-family homes built around ~150 acres of lakes, with floorplans ranging from roughly 2,000 to ~5,700 square feet. [verified — optimaproperties.com; floridahomefinder.com neighborhoods; langrealtynewhomes.com]
- **Parkland Bay** — Newer Lennar gated community of single-family homes arranged around a large central lake (~178 acres) with water views. [verified — optimaproperties.com; comeliveinflorida.com; homes.com guide]
- **Cypress Cay** — Gated single-family community (East and West sections) off Parkside Dr. south of Loxahatchee Rd., west of US-441, with community tennis court and pool. [verified — parkland-florida-realty.com subdivisions]
- **Ternbridge** — Gated single-family community (Ternbridge and Ternbridge Estates), ~198 homes on roughly 1/4–1/3-acre lots set among Parkland's protected woodlands. [verified — parkland-florida-realty.com subdivisions]
- **Heron Isles** — Single-family subdivision within the greater Heron Bay area. [verified — Heron Bay sub-community listings]
- **The Falls at Parkland** — 55-plus age-restricted gated community of single-family homes (~349 homes, by Ryan Homes), with a clubhouse/amenity center, pool, and fitness center. [verified — 55places.com/florida/communities/the-falls-at-parkland] *(factual age-restriction statement permitted; do not promotionally frame by age beyond the factual 55+ designation)*
- **BBB Ranches / The Ranches** — Original large-acreage ranch-style area reflecting the city's farming and equestrian origins. [verified — parklandhistoricalsociety / homesofparkland.com BBB Ranches]
- *(Also noted, factual age-restriction only:)* **Four Seasons at Parkland** — Gated 55-plus active-adult community (K. Hovnanian) with a clubhouse and scenic lakes. [verified — 55places.com/florida/communities/four-seasons-at-parkland]
