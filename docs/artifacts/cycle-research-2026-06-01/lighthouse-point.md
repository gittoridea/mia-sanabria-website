## Geography

Small coastal city in northeast Broward County, built almost entirely on a canal network dug during the 1950s-60s. It is bordered by Deerfield Beach to the north, Pompano Beach to the west and south, and the Intracoastal Waterway to the east (with Hillsboro Beach across the waterway). US-1 / Federal Highway forms the western boundary corridor and NE 24th Street the southern edge. Most residential streets touch a canal that connects to the Intracoastal Waterway, with Atlantic Ocean access via the nearby Hillsboro Inlet. **[verified]** — Wikipedia; City of Lighthouse Point; timsinger.com (boundaries: US-1 west, Intracoastal + Hillsboro Beach east, NE 24th St south).

> **County / coastal sanity check (explicit):** Lighthouse Point is correctly placed in **Broward County** (not Palm Beach or Miami-Dade) and is correctly described as **coastal** — it has direct Intracoastal Waterway frontage and ocean access via the Hillsboro Inlet. Both pass. **[verified]** — Wikipedia.

## Confirmed Durable Facts

2. It is a small coastal city covering roughly **2.39 square miles** (2.31 land / 0.085 water) in northeast Broward County. **[verified]** — Wikipedia. (Note: the city's own History page cites 2.36 sq mi as the post-1970-annexation land area; both figures are consistent within rounding.)

4. The vast majority of the city is built on **canals dug during the 1950s-60s**, creating extensive waterfront housing — **roughly 18 miles of navigable waterways**. **[verified]** — Wikipedia (canals 1950s-60s); visitlauderdale + jalexandergroup + gulfstreamboatclub (18 miles of navigable canals/waterways). The "18 miles" figure is consistent across multiple secondary sources but is not on a primary government page; treat as well-corroborated.
5. Atlantic in five minutes via Hillsboro Inlet"). No-fixed-bridge framing is a boating-source consensus, not a government statistic.
6. The city is **named for the Hillsboro Inlet Lighthouse**, located in adjacent **Hillsboro Beach**; the light was **first lit in March 1907**. **[verified]** — Wikipedia (Lighthouse Point + Hillsboro Inlet Light); USCG history. **NOTE:** do NOT publish a tower-height figure — the lighthouse tower is ~132 ft per Wikipedia, but other sources cite different heights (same height-ambiguity caveat as the Pompano Beach ledger).
