## Process improvements caught this cycle

1. **Concurrent xhigh codex calls have a rate cap.** Observed: 4 simultaneous Spark dispatches → first batch worked, second batch (4 more) stalled. Mitigation: dispatch in batches of ≤3 same-model concurrent; use `< /dev/null` for stdin. Documented in `CODEX_SPARK_CAPABILITY_PROBE.md`.
2. **`codex --sandbox read-only` cannot write files.** Even though the brief says "write to docs/codex-spark-audits/X.md," codex emits the audit content inline because the sandbox forbids file writes. Mitigation: use `<<AUDIT_START>> ... <<AUDIT_END>>` delimiters in the brief and extract from logs post-completion. Documented in skill spec.
3. **Codex CLI banner echoes the brief in its log.** When the brief contains structured headers (e.g. the audit template), the log has duplicate occurrences. Mitigation: extract the LAST/longest delimited block, not the first. Documented in extractor script.
4. **Honest model self-attestation isn't always feasible.** Team E (config: `gpt-5.4`) declined to claim the model identity in its evidence appendix. This is exemplary behavior — better than fabricated self-attestation. Future cycles should treat the `--config model=` flag as authoritative; team self-attestation is corroborating only.
5. **Live-staging "stale" claim from Team E was a transient state.** Team E fetched live staging mid-cycle and reported only 3 footer links visible; live verification post-cycle showed all 4 links. Likely Team E hit Caddy mid-cache-refresh. Lesson: trust the audit script's last-modified claim over a single agent fetch.
